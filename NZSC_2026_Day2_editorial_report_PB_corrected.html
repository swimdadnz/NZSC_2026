Six hundredths for gold, a Coast sweep — and a relay 0.79s from history.

What Day 2 says

Day 2 was less about one runaway performance than about compression at the top and depth underneath it. The men's 100 free gold was decided by just six hundredths, yet the same event also produced new 18-year and 14-year national marks and two more age-group swims within 0.34% of their records.

Coast supplied the clearest club storyline: a clean 1–2–3 in the women's 50 fly, then a 4×100 medley win that was simultaneously 15.01 seconds clear of second place and only 0.79 seconds short of the open NZ record. Across the individual programme, 43 of 60 top-ten NZ-eligible finalists improved from their heat, while the men's 400 IM stood out as the day's PB hotspot at 73.5%.

Methodology and data notes

The report uses Swimify Total summaries as canonical swim rows and Swimify age-band summaries for age-group medals. Day 2 is the local-date event block for 28 September 2026, corresponding to Sessions 3–4 and able-bodied events 11–18. Multi-class events are excluded. Entry is treated as pre-meet PB; PB analysis compares the fastest valid individual swim across all rounds on the day with the original first-round Entry. The record benchmark is the Swimming New Zealand short-course record sheet as at 23 September 2026. Record-setting swim counts update the applicable record chronologically, so Grayson Coulter's 25.34 in the 50 fly final is not counted as a second record because his 25.06 heat had already reset that mark. PB analysis includes able-bodied visitors for consistency with Day 1; NZ records and medals exclude visitors. The club PB comparison is displayed only for medal-winning clubs with at least five distinct Day 2 swimmers.
