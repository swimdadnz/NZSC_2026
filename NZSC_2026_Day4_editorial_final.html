2026 New Zealand Short Course Swimming Championships - Day 4

Coast swept the sprint backstroke and relays - while Tapper and the 200 free field hit Beijing pace.

What Day 4 says

Day 4 was the point where the meet's age-group record depth and its senior international pace converged. Amber George's 26.45 in the 50 back was an Open NZ record, Savannah-Eve Martin was only 0.03s behind, and both were inside the Beijing A standard. Milana Tapper then added a second individual A-standard event of her meet with 1:55.33 in the 200 free.

The men's 200 free supplied the age-group counterpart: Fraser Walker and Grayson Coulter each broke their age record in the morning and lowered it again at night, while the entire Open podium was inside the Beijing B standard. Coast finished the session with an Open NZ record in the women's 4x50 medley and victory in the men's relay. Across the individual programme, 357 of 648 swimmer-event opportunities produced a PB when the best valid swim of the day is compared with the original entry time.

Methodology and data notes

Day 4 is Sessions 7-8 and scheduled able-bodied events 29-38. Multi-class events and visitors are excluded from NZ records, qualifying-time counts, DQ/DNS audit and medal tallies. PB analysis includes able-bodied visitors for consistency with prior days. Entry from the first round is treated as the swimmer's pre-meet PB, and the fastest valid Day 4 swim across heat/final is compared with that original benchmark; updated final-round Entry values are not used as a new baseline. The pre-meet record benchmark is the Swimming New Zealand short-course record sheet as at 23 September 2026. Near misses use the record standing at the end of Day 4 and exclude swimmers/teams who actually broke that same record on Day 4. World Aquatics A/B standards are the official Beijing 2026 25m standards. The deferred men's 4x50 freestyle from Day 3 was re-swum during Session 7: its medals are included in the Session 7 NSS medal list and cumulative Open tally, but the race is excluded from Day 4 PB, record and DQ/DNS analysis.
