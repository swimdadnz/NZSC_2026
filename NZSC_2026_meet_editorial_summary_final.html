The stories that defined the meet

The selected editorial themes from the five daily reports, with the tables and visuals below carrying the rest of the statistical detail.
01
Records

Coulter: 10 record-breaking swims across six events

Grayson Coulter rewrote six separate 14-year records and produced 10 record-setting swims across the meet. The final one came as a 50.06 lead-off in the mixed 4x100 free on the last evening, lowering his own 100 free mark again.

02
Records

April Lin: record progression, then a five-hundredths miss

April Lin produced four record-breaking swims across three events. Her 13-year 200 breast progression ended at 2:28.90, 2.64% under the pre-meet record, and on Day 5 she missed the 13-year 200 IM record by just 0.05s.

03
Distance

William Tye bookended the meet with an 800/1500 record double

Tye opened the championships with a 15-year 1500 free record of 15:26.42 and closed with an 800 free record of 8:07.90. He was also only 0.26% outside the 15-year 200 free record on Day 4.

04
Backstroke

Milan Glintmeyer combined three age records with Beijing A pace

Glintmeyer broke 18-year records in the 100 back, 100 IM and 200 back. Her 57.35 100 back was also inside the Beijing A benchmark, making the record haul relevant at both age-group and international level.

05
Beijing

Six A-standard swims — all from Coast or St Peter’s

Five swimmers combined for six performances at or under the Beijing A standard. Coast supplied Milan Glintmeyer, Amber George and Savannah-Eve Martin; St Peter’s supplied Alyssa Tapper plus Milana Tapper’s 400 and 200 free performances.

06
Beijing

Milana Tapper was the only swimmer with A-standard times in two events

Tapper went 4:04.13 in the 400 free and 1:55.33 in the 200 free, producing two separate performances inside the Beijing A benchmarks over the five-day meet.

07
PBs

The male/female PB split persisted across the championship

After including eligible relay lead-off swims in each swimmer’s best meet time, men PB’d in 64.8% of valid swimmer-events versus 47.8% for women — a 17.0-point gap.

08
Poolside

Kevin Zhang’s 400 free produced the line of the meet

Zhang won the open men’s 400 free in 3:49.39. In poolside/interview context supplied after the race, he described the final 100m as “fighting for his life” and said he thought he “saw Jesus” as he pushed through the finish.
